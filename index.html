Final year thesis: NO2 over Lahore, 2021 to 2025

A team study of the spatial and temporal distribution of nitrogen dioxide over Lahore, done with Amna Amin and Awais Javaid under the supervision of Dr. Irfan Jalees at the Institute of Environmental Engineering and Research, UET Lahore.

Method

Sentinel-5P/TROPOMI data processed in Google Earth Engine for ten zones of Lahore, compared with Punjab EPA ground monitoring data. Maps made in QGIS. Statistics (ANOVA, Pearson correlation, RMSE, MAE) in Excel and Minitab.

Findings

Mughalpura, Samanabad, Model TownHighest NO2 zones
Shahdara, Iqbal TownCleanest zones
Winter high, summer lowClear seasonal pattern
r = 0.011Weak correlation between satellite and ground data
MAE 0.2545Overall mean absolute error
RMSE 0.3074Overall root mean square error

The weak agreement between satellite and ground data shows why remote sensing products need careful validation before they are used for decisions.
